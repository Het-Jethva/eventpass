# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

University-club Organizers are the primary users, configuring Events and managing registration and attendance. Check-in Volunteers are the high-pressure secondary users who need fast, unmistakable admission decisions on mobile devices; Attendees are occasional public users registering and presenting Tickets without accounts.

## Product Purpose

EventPass is a production-capable Event registration, signed-ticket, and attendance platform demonstrated with synthetic data. Success means an Organizer can configure an Event, an Attendee can register and receive a Ticket, and a Volunteer can admit them reliably despite intermittent connectivity, with conflicts and privileged changes remaining auditable.

## Positioning

Event check-in that stays trustworthy when the internet does not.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and dependable. EventPass should feel composed in a crowded check-in line and rigorous around state without resembling enterprise security software.

## Anti-references

Avoid dark cybersecurity dashboards, purple-gradient SaaS styling, oversized marketing metrics, and interfaces assembled entirely from floating rounded cards. EventPass should not resemble a crypto product or a generic dashboard template.

## Design Principles

- Make every admission state unmistakable, especially under time pressure.
- Communicate offline limitations and snapshot age honestly instead of implying false certainty.
- Give each role only the information and actions needed for its immediate job.
- Keep gate interactions fast while making destructive or privileged Organizer actions deliberate.
- Demonstrate production semantics with synthetic data; never mock the core workflow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Communicate scan outcomes through text, icons, color, and optional sound or vibration rather than color alone; provide at least 44px scanner touch targets, strong contrast for bright environments, keyboard and screen-reader support, and reduced-motion alternatives.
